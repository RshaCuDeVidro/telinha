Don't have it yet?

On Windows it's a single file, nothing to install:

{{icon:windows}}Download telinha .exe

Open the .exe once (Windows may ask you to "Run anyway") and come back here to click Open in telinha.

On Linux, one command (just for your user, no sudo):

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RshaCuDeVidro/telinha/main/install.sh | sh

Then come back here and click Open in telinha.

There's no phone app. If the room has the watch-in-browser page, ask whoever invited you for its link (the TV button in the room copies it).
